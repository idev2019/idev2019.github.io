Super Manager FC — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy applies to the iOS version of Super Manager FC, published by iDev (Jorge Anderson). The Android version is published separately and has its own policy.

Information we collect

Super Manager FC works without an account. Your careers, squads, settings and the manager name you type are stored only on your device. We do not run a server that receives your game data, and we do not ask for your email, contacts, location or any other personal information. If you uninstall the app or lose the device, your saved careers can be lost.

Purchases

The optional one-time PRO purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat (a purchase management service) to confirm that PRO is unlocked and to restore it. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and your App Store purchase information; it does not receive your name, email or payment details. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Internet connection check

The game requires an internet connection to start a session. To verify it, the app makes a request to Apple's connectivity test address (captive.apple.com). Apple receives the usual network request information, such as your IP address, under its own privacy policy. No game data is sent.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The iOS version of Super Manager FC does not show ads, does not include analytics or crash-reporting services, and does not track you across other companies' apps and websites. If this changes, we will update this page and the App Store privacy details before the change is released.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Game data lives on your device: delete a career inside the app or remove the app to erase it. Purchase records kept by Apple and RevenueCat can be handled through those providers or by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

Updates are posted on this page with a new date.
